About this board

Every word and phrase here is real. Each one comes from a music-technology press release — the announcements companies put out about new apps, tools, and deals.

I collected them one week at a time, between April and June 2026. I kept the lines that made me stop and look twice. Some sound almost human. Some describe music, and the people who make it, in strange ways.

This began as my project for a class at the School for Poetic Computation, called Rhythm Analytics.

How to use it

  • The left side is your word bank, sorted into objects, actions, qualities, and connectors. Use the toggle at the top to switch between single words and whole phrases.
  • Drag any tile onto the right side to add it to your poem. To remove a tile, drag it back to the left, or double-click it.
  • Not sure where to start? Press suggest a poem for a first draft, then change it however you want.
  • Clear canvas wipes the page so you can begin again.
  • Your poem stays in place when you switch between words and phrases, so you can mix the two.

Why it exists

The tools these press releases describe are made to be fast and automatic, and to work without much help from you. This board is the opposite. It is slow, it is made by hand, and it can only ever rearrange words the industry already said out loud.

Reading those words back as poetry is a way to hear what they actually mean.
